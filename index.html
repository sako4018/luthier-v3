За мен

Stepan is a delight to work with, even by email at long distance, and he does his best to fulfill all his clients' wishes.”

I am pleased to recommend you to anyone who wants a superior instrument at a very reasonable price.”

Tommy Kuo

Violinist, Redwood Symphony Orchestra, Redwood City, California
“Stepan — your 2002 Del Gesu copy easily matches if not outright best violins. In the 6 years that I have been playing it, it has opened up. As a new violin, it sounds ‘new’ to the player and to anybody 5–10 feet away, but from 20 feet or more away, it takes on another character. It becomes big, wide, and dark, and very smooth.”
